# FloodGuard AI — 90-second presentation script

Assalam-o-Alaikum. My project is **FloodGuard AI: NASA Rainfall Trend Monitor**.

The problem is that environmental data can be difficult for ordinary people to understand. A rainfall number on its own does not tell us whether the recent rainfall is unusual compared with the past.

FloodGuard AI makes NASA precipitation data easier to explore. A user selects a city, such as Faisalabad, and the app retrieves daily precipitation estimates from NASA POWER. It displays daily rainfall, monthly totals and annual trends.

The key feature is the seven-day comparison. The app compares the latest complete seven-day rainfall total with historical seven-day totals from the same calendar month. It then explains whether the recent rainfall is within the usual range or unusually wet. It also shows a long-term annual trend with a statistical p-value.

This project aligns with NASA's Earth System Trend Detective challenge because it visualizes environmental change over time and makes trend analysis more accessible.

This is not a flood prediction system. Flooding depends on river levels, drainage, soil, terrain and many other factors. Our current prototype focuses honestly on rainfall trends, and future work can integrate NASA GPM IMERG and local hydrological observations.

Thank you.
